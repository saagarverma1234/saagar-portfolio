How to play Protect Pumpkin!

Pumpkin is the orange tabby cat from the Swipe Theory channel, and things keep falling on him. Put your finger on the screen (or use your mouse) and move the shield to keep him safe. The shield floats a little above your finger so you can always see it.

The rules

  1. Keep your finger on the screen. If you lift it, the game waits for you.
  2. Block everything that comes at Pumpkin. Every hit costs part of a heart. You have 5 hearts.
  3. Let the golden fish through! Fish are Pumpkin's snack. If he catches one he gets half a heart back.
  4. Survive until the bar at the top is full. Lose at most 1 heart for 3 stars. Any star unlocks the next level.

The 8 worlds

  • Rainy Day, Hailstorm, Snowball Fight and Wild Wind, with a tornado at the end.
  • Meteor Shower (with a frying pan), Volcano Island, Alien Attack and finally Impossible, where a dragon throws everything at once.
  • The last level of every world ends with something giant falling on Pumpkin... and a twist. We won't spoil it.

Endless mode

Beat level 1-3 to unlock Endless. The worlds change every 25 seconds and it keeps getting faster. Your best score is saved on this device.

Tips

  • Watch where things come from: rain leans with the wind, snowballs and lava fly in arcs from the sides, aliens aim straight at Pumpkin.
  • Keep the shield close to Pumpkin's head. Small moves are faster than big ones.
  • On a computer you can also use the arrow keys or WASD. Press P to pause.

For parents

No account, no chat and nothing to download. Progress is saved only in this browser (local storage) and never sent anywhere. Ads below the game are set to non-personalized. Sound starts only after the first tap and can be muted with the speaker button.
