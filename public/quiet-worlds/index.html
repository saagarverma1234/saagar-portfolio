    How to play Choose Your Planet

    Every day at midnight UTC, four new planets drop, the same four for everyone. Each shows a picture and one cosy promise. Exactly one of them is safe. Pick the one you trust and live there for a whole year.

    1. Pick a planet. Tap a card to read it, then press the button to move in. Number keys 1 to 4 and Enter work too.
    2. Live through the year. Three times during the year (around day 40, day 150 and day 300) something happens and you choose what to do. Read closely: every planet gives you clues about its secret.
    3. See how your year ended. On day 365 you either thrived, survived the year or didn't make it. Then the planet tells you its secret, the rule that keeps you safe there, and how dangerous it really was.
    4. Compare. After every choice you see what share of players chose the same, once enough people have played.

    How endings work

    • The safe planet forgives everything. Follow its rule all year and you thrive. Slip up and you still survive.
    • Risky planets forgive one slip. Two and you don't make it.
    • Doomed planets forgive nothing. But a careful player who follows the rule every time can still make it through the year.

    Keepsakes

    Every planet hides one small keepsake. Reach a planet's best ending on the day it drops and the keepsake goes on your shelf, with a rarity (common, rare, epic or legendary) rolled once and kept forever. Your first planet each day is the one that counts; past planets and free play are for practice.

    Past planets and free play

    Missed a day? Every earlier day is under Past planets. Free play deals four random planets as often as you like and counts how many years in a row you survive.

    Tips

    • Too perfect is a warning sign. A promise that sounds flawless often is not. A world that already sounds a little odd is sometimes the gentle one.
    • Notes, signs and habits are clues. The planet usually tells you its rule if you pay attention.

    About Quiet Worlds

    Quiet Worlds is a YouTube channel of calm, slightly eerie "choose a planet to survive on for one year" videos. Every episode shows four little low-poly worlds, each with a cosy promise and a hidden secret. Most planets in this game come straight from the episodes, with their pictures rendered from the same 3D scenes, plus a few new worlds made just for the game.

    FAQ

    Is it free? Yes, with no download or account. Ads below the game help pay for it. There are no paid retries.

    Where is my progress saved? In your browser only (local storage). To move it to another phone, open More, then Save code. We only count anonymous plays and anonymous choices, so we can show what share of players chose each option (no cookies, no names). See the privacy policy.

    Can I put it on my home screen? Yes. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, open the menu and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.

    Is there sound? A soft ambient soundtrack is made live in your browser after your first tap. Use the speaker button to turn it off.
