    How to play Choose Your Planet

    Every round shows four planets, each with a picture and one cosy promise: a warm cottage, a full pantry, a friendly neighbour. You have to live on one of them for a whole year. Pick the one you trust. Then the planet tells you its secret, and you find out how your year went.

    1. Read the four promises. Tap a planet to select it, then press the button to move in. Number keys 1 to 4 and Enter work too.
    2. Watch the secret. Every world is hiding something. Some secrets are dangerous, some are only strange, and a few are the sweetest thing about the planet.
    3. See your year. You either thrive, barely survive, or don't make it. The danger meter shows how risky the planet really was, and the rule tells you how people survive there.
    4. Keep going. A full game is ten rounds. Your score counts every year you survived, and your streak counts years in a row.

    Daily challenge

    The daily challenge is five rounds, and everybody in the world gets the same planets that day (it changes at midnight UTC). Play it once, share your result and compare with friends.

    Tips

    • Every round has exactly one safe planet. It is not always the one that sounds the nicest.
    • Too perfect is a warning sign. A promise that sounds flawless often is not. A world that already sounds a little odd is sometimes the gentle one.
    • Learn the rules. Many planets can be survived if you follow one simple rule. The reveal always tells you what it was.

    About Quiet Worlds

    Quiet Worlds is a YouTube channel of calm, slightly eerie "choose a planet to survive on for one year" videos. Every episode shows four little low-poly worlds, each with a cosy promise and a hidden secret, and the comments argue about which one is really safe. Most planets in this game come straight from the episodes, with their pictures rendered from the same 3D scenes, plus a few new worlds made just for the game.

    FAQ

    Is it free? Yes, with no download or account. Ads below the game help pay for it.

    Where is my score saved? In your browser only (local storage): your best score, streak and which planets you have already seen, so new games show you new worlds first. Nothing is sent to a server; see the privacy policy.

    Is there sound? A soft ambient soundtrack is made live in your browser after your first tap. Use the speaker button to turn it off.
