How to play

Drag gates, walls and bumpers from the tray onto the board. Tap a piece to turn it, drag it back to the tray to remove it. Press Drop: every ball that goes through a xN gate becomes N balls (each ball can use each gate once). Lava eats balls. Beat the target for a star, and reach the 3-star score to master the level.

The counter is exact: it is 1 ball times every split, minus the balls lost in lava. Sound is made live in your browser.
